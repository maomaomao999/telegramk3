## Description

Kislap helps people learn together.

Find nearby partners who share your learning goals, whether you want to practice English, improve your programming skills, learn music or singing, or teach something you already know.

LEARN

Choose a skill and discover learning partners using approximate distance ranges. Nearby visibility is off until you turn it on, and it expires automatically.

TEACH

Create a learning profile that shows what you can teach, what you want to learn and when you are available.

CONNECT

Send a learning request, agree on a goal and continue the conversation with messages, groups, voice calls or video calls.

SAFETY BY DESIGN

- Approximate area instead of an exact position
- Mutual connection before Kislap conversation tools open
- Block and report controls
- Dating is separate, optional and off by default

Kislap combines focused learning discovery with reliable communication so a new study connection can become an ongoing learning partnership.
